## ⚡ Active Interactions & Motion

1. **Infinite Logo Ticker**: Continuous GPU-accelerated 60FPS marquee scrolling. Pauses on hover.
2. **Interactive Hero App Mockup**:
   - Click on projects in the sidebar (*Ace Chemicals, Janus Cosmetics, Stagg Enterprises*) to switch workspace context and re-target the studio engine.
   - Click on the prompt composer to simulate live generation prompts with instant feedback.
3. **Interactive FAQ Accordion**: Click any question in the FAQ list to expand the answer with smooth transition.
4. **Customer Stories Carousel**: Click the Previous / Next arrows to slide through client testimonials.
5. **Interactive Contact Form**: Live validation and confirmation toast.
