Our story

A new way to craft digital experiences

Websites used to be generic templates, slow loads, and rigid builders.
Modern craft is breaking that mold, and we are building what comes next.

We started Wrapper Studio because we were exhausted by traditional creative agencies: six-month bloated timelines, junior designers assigned after the pitch, and static Figma mockups that engineer poorly. We replaced that broken model with high-velocity studio sprints, direct collaboration with senior creative directors, and production-ready Next.js code.

Since 2022, Wrapper Studio has partnered with over 140+ venture-backed startups and category leaders—spanning San Francisco, New York, London, and Tokyo—shipping web experiences that have raised over .2B in venture capital and won 18 Awwwards and FWA honors.

We are a boutique collective of senior creative directors, interaction designers, and frontend engineers. We intentionally cap our client engagements to 3 concurrent sprints at a time, ensuring every project receives obsession-level craft, pixel-perfection, and senior attention.
